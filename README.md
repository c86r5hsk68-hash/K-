# KÔ — The Intelligent Financial

> KÔ helps people understand their money, manage it better, and use it to build their future.

An AI-powered personal finance app designed for young people and African users. Enter your income, expenses, and goals — KÔ analyses your situation and shows you where your money is going, how to cut expenses, how much you can save, and how to reach your goals faster.

## ✨ Features

- **Financial Score** — a 0–100 rating of your money habits, updated as you improve
- **Smart Advice** — KÔ analyses your biggest expenses and suggests realistic savings
- **Income & Expense Tracking** — simple, mobile-friendly entry
- **Goals** — set a target and see how long until you reach it
- **Savings Challenges** — habit-building tasks that boost your score
- **Reviews & Suggestions** — users can leave feedback directly in the app
- **Works Offline** — installable on your phone like a native app (PWA)

## 🧪 Try It

Add an income of `1500 GHS`, then these expenses:

| Category | Amount |
|---|---|
| Food | 500 |
| Outings | 300 |
| Impulse purchases | 200 |
| Transport | 200 |

KÔ will flag your outings and impulse spending and show how much you could save.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build step
- Data stored in your browser via `localStorage`
- Hosted free on GitHub Pages
- PWA-enabled (installable, works offline)
